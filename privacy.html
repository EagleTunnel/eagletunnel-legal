Privacy Policy for Eagle Tunnel

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Eagle Tunnel is a VPN tunnel management app for Android, iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This privacy policy explains what information the app accesses, uses, and shares.

Information We Collect

Eagle Tunnel does not automatically collect or send personal information to the developer.

The app stores VPN profiles and configuration data locally on your device. These profiles may include server addresses, keys, credentials, and other VPN connection settings that you import or create.

Permissions and Local Access

Depending on your device, Eagle Tunnel may request the following access:

  • Camera (Android, iPhone, and iPad): used only to scan QR codes for importing VPN profiles.
  • Files selected by you: used only to import profiles or QR code images, or save exported profiles and diagnostic logs.
  • VPN and network access: used to create, manage, and monitor VPN tunnel connections.
  • Boot completed (Android): used to restore tunnel state when that behavior is enabled.
  • System overlay or quick settings features (Android): used only where required by Android for VPN-related controls.

How VPN Traffic Works

When you enable a VPN tunnel, your network traffic is routed according to the VPN profile you selected. The developer of Eagle Tunnel does not inspect, collect, or store your browsing activity, DNS queries, or network traffic. Your chosen VPN provider operates the server and has its own privacy practices.

Data Storage

VPN profiles, settings, and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your device. Apple clients use Keychain to store credentials. You can delete profiles in the app. Uninstalling may leave Apple Keychain items or system VPN configurations; remove VPN configurations in system settings when needed.

Data Sharing

Eagle Tunnel does not sell user data. Eagle Tunnel does not share user data with advertisers or analytics providers.

Exported profiles may contain private keys and credentials. Diagnostic logs may contain profile names and server addresses. Files are saved or shared only when you choose to export or share them.

Third-Party Services

Eagle Tunnel does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Data Security

Eagle Tunnel uses system APIs and VPN functionality to manage tunnels. You are responsible for importing VPN profiles only from providers or servers you trust.

Children's Privacy

Eagle Tunnel is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the support contact information provided on the Eagle Tunnel listing in the app store for your platform (such as Google Play or the Apple App Store).

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted at this same URL.
